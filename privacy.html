Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2025 · Truellion Technologies · AutoSend for Gmail™

Truellion Technologies is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information AutoSend for Gmail™ ("the Add-on") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you are added to the Add-on whitelist, we store your Gmail address solely to verify access authorization. During use, the following data is temporarily processed within Google's infrastructure:

  • Recipient email addresses you enter
  • Email subject and body content you compose
  • Your selected delay settings
  • Email queue state (deleted after batch completes)

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Gmail™ password or credentials
  • Copies of emails you send
  • Device information, IP addresses, or browser data
  • Analytics data of any kind
  • Payment information

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Verifying your Gmail™ address is authorized
  • Processing your email send queue
  • Sending completion notifications
  • Providing customer support

4. Data Storage & Infrastructure

The Add-on is built entirely on Google's official cloud infrastructure. We do not operate external servers or databases. All data lives within Google's ecosystem via Google Apps Script™ PropertiesService and Google OAuth 2.0™.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your Gmail address is retained only while your access is active. To request deletion, email zaavian@truellion.com with subject: AutoSend Data Deletion Request. We process requests within 7 business days.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Add-on's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
