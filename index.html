Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2025  ·  Published by Truellion Technologies  ·  Applies to: AutoSend for Gmail™

Truellion Technologies is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information AutoSend for Gmail™ ("the Add-on") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: When you are added to the Add-on whitelist by an administrator, we store your Gmail address solely to verify access authorization.

Information processed during use: When you send emails, the following data is temporarily processed within Google's infrastructure:

  • Recipient email addresses you enter into the Add-on interface
  • Email subject and body content you compose
  • Your selected delay settings
  • Email queue state (stored temporarily in Google Apps Script™ PropertiesService)

This data is deleted automatically once your email batch is fully sent.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Gmail™ password or account credentials
  • Copies of emails you send
  • Device information, IP addresses, or browser data
  • Analytics data (no Google Analytics, Firebase, or similar tools)
  • Payment information of any kind

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Verifying your Gmail™ address is authorized to use the Add-on
  • Processing your email send queue with your configured time delays
  • Sending you a completion notification when your batch finishes
  • Providing customer support when you contact us

4. Data Storage & Infrastructure

The Add-on is built entirely on Google's official cloud infrastructure. We do not operate external servers or databases. All data lives within Google's ecosystem:

  • Whitelist stored in Google Sheets™ or Google Apps Script™ PropertiesService
  • Email queue stored temporarily in Apps Script™ PropertiesService — deleted after batch completion
  • Authentication handled entirely by Google OAuth 2.0™ — we never see or store your Google password

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your data only interacts with Google's own services (Gmail API™, Apps Script™), which are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your Gmail address is retained on the whitelist only while your access remains active. To request deletion, email zaavian@truellion.com with the subject: AutoSend Data Deletion Request. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Note: Uninstalling the Add-on from your Google account also removes all locally stored Add-on data automatically.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Add-on's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only request the minimum OAuth scopes necessary to perform email sending on your behalf.

AutoSend button not appearing in Gmail™?

Complete the installation by clicking the install link and approving all OAuth permissions. Refresh Gmail™ (press F5). If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall the Add-on from Google Workspace Marketplace™.

Emails going to spam?

Use Safe Mode (5–10 minute delays) for large lists. Avoid spam trigger words in your subject or body. Start with small batches to build sender reputation.
